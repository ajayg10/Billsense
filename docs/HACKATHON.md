# Hackathon handoff

## Demo outline (90 seconds)

- 0–15s: Explain the beginner's problem: a billing export has numbers but little context.
- 15–35s: Open sample report and show net cost, credits, and service breakdown.
- 35–55s: Open the largest finding's evidence drawer and inspect exact CSV records.
- 55–70s: Show previous-period comparison and investigation checklist.
- 70–90s: Explain Decimal calculations, FastAPI on AWS, optional AI fallback, and the privacy choices.

The sample is synthetic. Current USD 370.50, previous USD 220.70, difference USD 149.80. Do not present it as actual savings or real customer data.

Bedrock in the product does not itself demonstrate the separate coding-agent connection requirement. No such evidence has been fabricated or claimed for this build.

## Suggested positioning

BillSense helps beginner cloud builders understand billing exports through reproducible calculations and visible evidence. Each finding links to source records, and AI explanations are optional. The project does not promise automatic savings or resource-level diagnosis.
